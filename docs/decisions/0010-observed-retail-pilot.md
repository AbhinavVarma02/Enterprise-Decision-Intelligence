# ADR 0010 · Preserve the synthetic study and publish the observed-data pilot separately

Accepted 2026-11-26.

The synthetic study supplies known latent demand for controlled evaluation and existing browser decision cases. The official FreshRetailNet dataset supplies real stockout annotations and demand observations during available periods. Genuine stockout demand and store economics remain unobserved.

Use a pinned 60-series retail pilot with its own data directory, research output and web export. Fit selection and volume scaling on days 1–69, remove target-day promotion inputs, and verify the publisher's original temporal partitions. Preserve the synthetic article and artifacts for historical reproduction. Compare the studies descriptively because their configurations and settlement rules differ.

The corrected research harness records breaches on submitted orders and settles only feasible quantities under authoritative terms. Matching validator and reviewer draws across policy variants controls stochastic differences in the ablations. Run the selective routing experiment separately with a fixed rule and independent scenario seed. Both changes and their interpretations are specified in [the protocol](../EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md).

The pilot supports a bounded comparison with observed sales and stockout conditions. Its bootstrap intervals cover series in one week and its financial conclusions remain conditional on modeled demand and operations. The public site serves separate precomputed evidence and synthetic decision cases.
